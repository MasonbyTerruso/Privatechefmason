Frequently Asked Questions

Where are your services available?

Primarily based between Turin, Milan, the French Riviera, and Monaco. Available worldwide for international travel, private villa/yacht tenures, and exclusive events.

Every menu and meal plan is fully customized around athletic goals, medical guidelines, allergies, or personal preferences. Strict confidentiality and NDAs are guaranteed at all times.
