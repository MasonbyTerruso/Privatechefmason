PRACTICAL INFO

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking a private chef service or performance nutrition consultation.

Where do you operate and deliver services?

I operate primarily across Turin, Milan, the French Riviera, and Monaco. I am also available for national and international travel, villa/yacht private tenure, and global private events.

How far in advance should I book?

For private dinners and exclusive events, booking 7-14 days in advance is recommended. For seasonal performance chef services or ongoing athlete support, scheduling is arranged via dedicated agreements.

How do you handle dietary restrictions and privacy?

Every menu or meal plan is custom-balanced for medical needs, specific athletic diets, allergies, or ethical preferences. Strict confidentiality and NDAs are standard across all engagements.

What is included in the home private chef service?

The service includes sourcing prime ingredients, on-site preparation in your kitchen, table service, and complete clean-up and sanitization of the workspace.
